UI tour

What agency staff see after startup

These generated browser captures show the intended private Operations Console flow. They are documentation aids, not retained evidence and not proof of external approval.

/admin/operations

Start Here

Use it for: setup progress, first-run tasks, feed URLs, and next actions.

/admin/operations/gtfs-workbench

GTFS Workbench

Use it for: required files, row counts, service dates, import history, and validation issue triage.

/admin/operations/feed-health

Feed Health

Use it for: `feeds.json`, schedule, Vehicle Positions, Trip Updates, and Alerts review.

/admin/operations/realtime

Realtime Center

Use it for: Vehicle Positions usefulness, Trip Updates withholding, Alerts lifecycle review, and synthetic replay guidance.

/admin/operations/connectors

Connector Hub

Use it for: telemetry, prediction, validator, monitoring/export, and discovery connector choices.

/admin/operations/readiness

Readiness

Use it for: local CAL-ITP-style readiness review and what each signal does not prove.

Tour Script

  1. Open /admin/operations and identify the first next action.
  2. Open GTFS Workbench and explain active schedule status.
  3. Open Feed Health and review the five feed paths.
  4. Open Realtime Center and explain withheld or stale outputs.
  5. Open Connector Hub and choose the closest connector category.
  6. Open Readiness and read the "does not prove" language before any outside claim.
