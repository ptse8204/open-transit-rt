Video tutorial workflow

Record short demos from the same browser-first path.

This page gives maintainers a public-safe outline for recording walkthroughs after a local demo app starts. Do not commit large video files here; publish finished media only through an authorized release or docs workflow.

3 minutes

Overview

Show what Open Transit RT is, who it is for, Start Here, the five feed paths, connectors, readiness, and what local review does not prove.

10 minutes

Local setup

Show a technical helper starting the app, then switch to browser-only agency review.

6 minutes

GTFS import

Open GTFS Import, GTFS Workbench, GTFS Quality, and Validation Center.

6 minutes

Feed health and readiness

Review feed health, validation health, readiness areas, and consumer preparedness limits.

5 minutes

Connector and AVL overview

Show connector categories, workbench recipes, and authenticated telemetry boundary.

5 minutes

Maintenance and support

Show Maintenance, Help, support bundle guidance, and what requires a technical helper.
