Self-hosted GTFS and GTFS Realtime

Open Transit RT

A browser-first, open-source transit data backend for small agencies. After a technical helper starts the app, agency staff can review setup, import GTFS, check feeds, connect vehicle data, and review readiness from the Operations Console.

Try Locally See The UI Tour Browse Connectors

Local/self-hosted evaluation does not prove CAL-ITP/Caltrans compliance, production readiness, agency adoption, consumer acceptance, hosted service availability, vendor compatibility, SLA/uptime, production AVL reliability, or ETA quality.
