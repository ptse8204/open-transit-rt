Schedule Import Flow

  1. Choose a GTFS ZIPUse public-safe or authorized schedule data. Keep private agency files out of public demos.
  2. Import or reviewConfirm required files, row counts, service dates, routes, stops, trips, and active feed version.
  3. Review validationUse internal import validation and configured canonical validator records when available.
  4. Check feed URLsAfter the active schedule exists, review the public schedule path and realtime outputs.

Administrator needed when

  • The ZIP is missing or too large.
  • Database migrations are not current.
  • Validator tooling is unavailable.
  • Rollback or table-level review is needed.

Limits

A successful local import does not prove source correctness, agency approval, validator-clean public deployment, consumer acceptance, compliance, production readiness, or ETA quality.
