CAL-ITP-style readiness

Review readiness without overclaiming it.

Open Transit RT helps operators check the pieces commonly needed for public GTFS and GTFS Realtime workflows. The local review gives next actions; it does not prove compliance, production readiness, or consumer acceptance.

Public Feed URLs

Check feeds.json, static GTFS, Vehicle Positions, Trip Updates, and Alerts URLs.

Static GTFS

Check import state, active feed version, required files, service dates, and validation issues.

Vehicle Positions

Check telemetry freshness, assignment confidence, stale vehicles, and publishable rows.

Trip Updates

Check prediction source, generated or withheld output, low confidence, ambiguity, and stale telemetry.

Alerts

Check Alerts URL, lifecycle guidance, stale alerts, and missing disruption links.

Validation

Check allowlisted validators, latest records, stale results, blockers, and next actions.

License And Contact

Check open-license fields and technical-contact metadata.

Operations Signals

Check feed health, reliability, maintenance, backup/restore guidance, and support paths.

Telemetry And Devices

Check device bindings, accepted observations, stale handling, and simulator guidance.

Consumer Preparedness

Check draft packet records and public feed metadata without changing consumer status.

Before Outside Claims

Public feed review

Collect public HTTPS fetch proof, official feed listing review, validator results, and agency-approved license/contact metadata in a deployment-specific evidence workflow.

Consumer or aggregator status

Do not move draft packet records without separately authorized, retained target-originated evidence.

Production or SLA language

Operational monitoring, backups, restore practice, incident handling, and service commitments are deployment-owned work.

Allowed wording: Open Transit RT supports CAL-ITP-style readiness workflows. It does not prove compliance, production readiness, consumer acceptance, vendor compatibility, or ETA quality without future retained evidence.
