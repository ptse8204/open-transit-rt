Current boundary

Status

Open Transit RT has a broad local product foundation, but external adoption, consumer, final-root, compliance, hosted-service, vendor, SLA, and ETA-quality claims remain evidence-limited.

What works for local evaluation

  • GTFS import and GTFS Studio draft workflows.
  • Public feed paths for schedule, Vehicle Positions, Trip Updates, Alerts, and discovery metadata.
  • Authenticated telemetry ingest and conservative matching.
  • Private Operations Console pages for readiness, validation, feed health, connectors, and simulator guidance.

What is not claimed

  • CAL-ITP/Caltrans compliance.
  • Consumer submission, review, acceptance, ingestion, listing, or display.
  • Agency adoption, endorsement, approval, or final-root proof.
  • Hosted SaaS, paid support, SLA, production readiness, vendor compatibility, hardware certification, or production-grade ETA quality.
Optional evidence tracks.

Real pilots, final-root proof, consumer submission, vendor proof, compliance evidence, and real-world ETA proof require explicit maintainer authorization, exact scope, public-safe retention rules, redaction rules, and stop conditions before work starts.
