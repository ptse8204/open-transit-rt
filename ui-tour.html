UI tour

Start from actions, not route lists.

After a technical helper starts the local app, staff open the local sign-in page and use the private Operations Console without tokens, curl, DevTools, or browser extensions. These captures are documentation aids, not retained evidence or proof of external approval.

/admin/operations

Start Here

Use it for: next actions, current status, feed URLs, and help.

/admin/operations/gtfs-workbench

GTFS Workbench

Use it for: required files, row counts, service dates, import history, and validation issues.

/admin/operations/feed-health

Feed Health

Use it for: feeds.json, schedule, Vehicle Positions, Trip Updates, and Alerts review.

/admin/operations/realtime

Realtime

Use it for: Vehicle Positions, withheld Trip Updates, Alerts, and replay guidance.

/admin/operations/connectors

Connector Hub

Use it for: telemetry, prediction, validator, monitoring, and discovery connector choices.

/admin/operations/readiness

Readiness

Use it for: local CAL-ITP-style readiness review and what each signal does not prove.

Tour Order

  1. Open /admin/local-login, choose Start setup, and land on /admin/operations.
  2. Use Start, Setup, GTFS, Feeds, Realtime, Vehicles, Connectors, Readiness, Maintenance, and Help.
  3. Open GTFS Workbench and check active schedule status.
  4. Open Feed Health and check the five feed paths.
  5. Open Realtime and review withheld or stale outputs.
  6. Open Connector Hub and choose the closest connector category.
  7. Open Readiness before making outside claims.
