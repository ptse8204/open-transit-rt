Self-hosted GTFS and GTFS Realtime

Open Transit RT

A browser-first, open-source transit data backend for small agencies. A technical helper starts the app once; agency staff sign in locally and use action pages to import GTFS, check feeds, connect vehicles, and review readiness.

Start Local Trial Watch Tutorial Tour The UI

Local/self-hosted evaluation does not prove CAL-ITP/Caltrans compliance, production readiness, agency adoption, consumer acceptance, hosted service availability, vendor compatibility, SLA/uptime, production AVL reliability, or ETA quality.
