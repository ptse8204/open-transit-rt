Browser-First Flow

Follow this order before interpreting readiness or making deployment decisions.

Start in the browser

Open the private Operations Console URL from a technical helper.

Open Agency Operations Cockpit / Start Here

Route: /admin/operations. Review the two paths, first-run tasks, five feed URLs, and boundaries.

Review setup

Use setup wizard and publication metadata pages to see what is configured or missing.

Import or review GTFS

Route: /admin/operations/gtfs-import. Upload a GTFS ZIP, import a safe URL, or inspect active schedule state.

Check five feed URLs

Route: /admin/operations/feed-health. Check exactly five public feed paths and next actions.

Review operations surfaces

Open Feed Health, Readiness, GTFS Quality, Validation Health, Device Credentials, Telemetry Freshness, Telemetry Simulator, Connector Hub, Connector Tests, and Maintenance Center.

Understand what remains

Use Operations Console Help and claim boundaries before making deployment or stronger public claims.
